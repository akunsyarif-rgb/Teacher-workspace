# Privacy Policy — Teacher Workspace Backup

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

Teacher Workspace Backup is a personal utility used by the owner to create encrypted backups of Firestore data and store those backup files in the owner's Google Drive account.

## Information Processed

The utility processes information stored in the owner's Firestore database only to create backups and support data recovery.

## Google Drive Access

The utility uses Google OAuth authorization to upload and manage backup files created by the utility in the owner's Google Drive account. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the owner.

## Data Protection

Backup files are encrypted before upload. The encryption key is controlled by the owner. The backup cannot be decrypted without the correct key.

## Data Sharing

The utility does not sell backup data or use it for advertising. Backup contents are stored in the owner's Google Drive account.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files are retained according to the configured backup retention policy. The owner may remove backup files from Google Drive.
